# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to contributors of every level of experience.
- Give feedback on the work, with a reason attached.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that "no" can be a complete
  answer when it comes with an explanation.
- Respect that everyone here is spending time they didn't have to spend.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report problems through GitHub's private reporting or by opening an issue if the
matter is not sensitive. Maintainers will review, respond, and may edit, hide or
delete comments, or block accounts, as they judge appropriate. Reporters' privacy
will be respected.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.
